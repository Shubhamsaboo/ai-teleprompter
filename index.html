AI Teleprompter

A teleprompter that listens and scrolls as you speak.

Fastest way to a script: Copy AI prompt, paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini with your draft or notes, and it returns a ready-to-load .md teleprompter script.

Script format — plain Markdown
# SECTION NAME     section banner (not spoken)
## Heading         spoken subheading
Plain lines        spoken text — one thought per line reads best
**bold**           punched word
[PAUSE] [SLOW]     pacing cues — chips, not spoken
[SCREEN: b-roll]   visual cue for your edit

…or drag any .md / .txt file anywhere in this window

Works in Chrome and Safari — other browsers don't ship the speech engine that voice tracking needs.
